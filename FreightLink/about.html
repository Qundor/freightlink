ABOUT FREIGHTLINK

WE'RE BUILDING
THE NETWORK.

FreightLink is a Zimbabwean-born trucking and freight network connecting the people, businesses and opportunities that keep goods moving.

OUR IDEA

A more connected
trucking industry.

FreightLink was created around a simple idea: trucking works better when the right people can find each other.

Initially focused on Zimbabwe and designed with a wider Southern African vision, FreightLink is building a digital environment for shippers, transporters, fleet owners, drivers and logistics professionals.
